Porsche 963

The Vanguard of Porsche's Modern Hybrid Prototype Era

Class: LMDh / GTP Hypercar Category

The Porsche 963 marks a triumphal return to top-flight international endurance prototype racing. Designed to compete simultaneously in the FIA World Endurance Championship (WEC) and the IMSA WeatherTech SportsCar Championship under the unifying LMDh regulations, the 963 is a cutting-edge hybrid weapon engineered in collaboration with chassis specialist Multimatic and operated by the legendary Porsche Penske Motorsport squad.

"The Porsche 963 embodies our commitment to the future of motorsport, bridging high-efficiency hybrid electrification with the unyielding performance of Porsche's racing heritage."

Beneath its muscular, wind-tunnel-refined bodywork beats a high-revving 4.6-liter twin-turbocharged V8 engine, structurally derived from the legendary 918 Spyder hybrid hypercar. This powerful internal combustion engine operates seamlessly alongside a standardized electric hybrid system, delivering instantaneous power and incredible efficiency to meet strict global constraints.

Race Results

Conquering Daytona and Dominating the Global Stage

Entering service at the dawn of the hybrid prototype renaissance, the Porsche 963 quickly unlocked its true potential, asserting structural supremacy across both sides of the Atlantic.

Its breakthrough crowning achievement arrived at the prestigious Rolex 24 at Daytona, where the #7 factory Porsche Penske Motorsport car claimed a monumental overall victory, marking Porsche’s first overall win at Daytona since 2003. Beyond its classic Florida triumph, the 963 prototype platform continues to accumulate dominant milestones, capturing multiple WEC and IMSA championship titles, pole positions, and outright race victories worldwide, cementing its position in a golden era of sports prototype racing.
