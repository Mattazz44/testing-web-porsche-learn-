Porsche 935 Tribute (2019)

Homage To ‘Moby Dick’ Race Car – The New Porsche 935

Official Premiere: September 27, 2018, Rennsport Reunion, Laguna Seca, USA

The 935 tribute was a non-street-legal collector’s car built in a limited series of only 77 units. It was based on the 911 GT2 RS high-performance sports car, equipped with its state-of-the-art 3.8-litre six-cylinder twin-turbo engine. The bodywork is a modern interpretation of the legendary Porsche 935/78 Le Mans race car, famously nicknamed "Moby Dick" for its elongated shape and massive fairings.

"This spectacular car is a birthday present from Porsche Motorsport to fans all over the world."

Like a true race car, it comes standard with a driver’s seat, a welded-in safety cage, an air jack system, and a fire extinguishing system. The aerodynamics are a completely new development, with many exterior details paying tribute to winning vehicles from Porsche's motor racing history.

Race Results

2019 Porsche Motorsport GT2 Supersportscar Weekend at Spa-Francorchamps

Held on July 26 and 27, 2019, the “Porsche Motorsport GT2 Supersportscar Weekend” was a one-off event where the new 935 made its competitive debut. Driver Egidio Perfetti masterfully piloted his 935 to victory in both 30-minute races, showcasing the car's incredible performance right out of the box against a field of 911 GT2 RS Clubsport cars.
