Porsche 911 Turbo S

The Timeless Yardstick of High-Performance Supercars

Class: High-Performance Sports Car / Supercar

Since its inception in 1963, the Porsche 911 has remained the absolute architectural gold standard for sports cars worldwide. In its current 992-generation flagship iteration—the 911 Turbo S—it represents the ultimate pinnacle of daily-driven usability paired with earth-shattering, hypercar-shaming acceleration. It stubbornly evolves its signature rear-engine silhouette while constantly incorporating state-of-the-art chassis electronics.

"The 911 is the only car that you can drive from an African safari to Le Mans, then to the theater and onto the streets of New York."
— Ferry Porsche

Nestled low behind the rear axle rests a masterpiece of internal combustion engineering: a 3,745 cc twin-turbocharged boxer engine. Channelling power through a lightning-fast 8-speed Porsche Doppelkupplung (PDK) transmission and a variable all-wheel-drive system, it generates continuous, physics-defying momentum. Active aerodynamics, including an adjustable front spoiler and extending rear wing, work in real-time harmony to anchor the vehicle to the tarmac.

Performance Legacy

Unrivaled Engineering Evolution

While competition continuously alters their platforms, the 911 retains its identity, constantly tuning and sharpening its distinct rear-engined characteristics over six decades.

The 992 Turbo S features a significantly wider track and enhanced Porsche Active Suspension Management (PASM) to completely neutralize body roll through high-speed curves. Armed with standard carbon-ceramic brakes (PCCB) and rear-axle steering, it blends raw structural aggression with elite grand touring luxury, making it an immortal icon on both public alpine passes and demanding track circuits like the Nürburgring Nordschleife.
