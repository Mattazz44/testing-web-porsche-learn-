TRUE RACING HERITAGE

BORN ON THE TRACK.IMMORTALIZED AS LEGENDS.

From the brutal sands of Paris-Dakar to the absolute supremacy on the sacred asphalt of Le Mans.

1984 - 1986

Conquering the Unforgiving Desert

Porsche proved to the world that high-performance sports cars are not just masters of the track. Through the Porsche 953 and the legendary 959 supercar, they dominated the most grueling competition on earth: the **Paris-Dakar Rally**. The advanced all-wheel-drive (AWD) system, tested under the torturous African sands, became the ultimate foundation for the iconic AWD technology driving today's commercial Porsche 911s.

1970 - 1971

The 917 Monster & The First Throne

The deafening roar of the Flat-12 engine, death-defying radical aerodynamics, and limitless bravery birthed Porsche's first absolute victory at the 24 Hours of Le Mans. The Porsche 917 didn't just win a race; it shattered distance records that stood for decades, establishing a visceral dominance that changed the face of global endurance racing forever.

1982 - 1987

The Golden Era of Group C

The golden age of mechanical engineering gave rise to the masterpiece Porsche 956 and its successor, the 962. Utilizing innovative aluminum monocoque chassis and ground-effect aerodynamics, this combat fleet ruthlessly swept world championships. An insane benchmark was set when Stefan Bellof blitzed the terrifying Nürburgring Nordschleife in 6 minutes and 11 seconds with the Porsche 956—a legendary lap time that remained unbroken for 35 years.

MODERN ERA

The Symphony of Hybrids & Hypercars

Porsche reclaimed Le Mans glory for three consecutive years (2015–2017) through the technological marvel of electrification in the Porsche 919 Hybrid. Today, that competitive fire burns within the heart of the **Porsche 963 LMDh/Hypercar**. Mating the brute force of a Twin-Turbo V8 with the thrust of a racing hybrid system, Porsche continues to lead modern motorsport civilization at the highest echelon.
