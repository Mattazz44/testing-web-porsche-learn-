Porsche 911 RSR

The Screaming King of GT Endurance Racing

Class: LM GTE / GTLM Category

The Porsche 911 RSR stands as one of the most revolutionary and acoustically iconic GT endurance race cars ever constructed. To maximize aerodynamic efficiency and accommodate a massive rear diffuser, Porsche Motorsport executed a radical architectural shift: shifting the legendary flat-six engine forward of the rear axle, effectively creating a mid-engine 911 platform built solely to dominate world-class sports car racing.

"The raw, unadulterated scream of the naturally aspirated flat-six at 9,000 RPM is a mechanical symphony that defined an unforgettable era of motorsport."

Equipped with a highly refined naturally aspirated 4.2-liter flat-six engine, the 911 RSR prioritized linear throttle response and bulletproof mechanical reliability over forced induction. Its ultra-aggressive aero package, combined with a quick-shift sequential gearbox, made it exceptionally fast through high-speed corners and a fierce opponent on track.

Race Results

GTE-Pro Mastery at Le Mans and Beyond

Throughout its operational tenure in the FIA World Endurance Championship (WEC) and IMSA SportsCar Championship, the 911 RSR achieved monumental success, building an unparalleled competitive legacy in the elite GTE-Pro field.

Its crowning achievement came at the 2018 24 Hours of Le Mans, where the factory squad captured a stunning 1-2 finish in the GTE-Pro class. The race car famously secured victory wearing the iconic historic "Pink Pig" heritage livery, closely followed by the sister car wrapped in the legendary "Rothmans" theme. The platform concluded its factory career in style, capturing the final GTE-Pro class victory at the 2022 24 Hours of Le Mans, cementing its name permanently into the history books of endurance racing.
